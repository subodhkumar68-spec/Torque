Entrance Exam Roadmap

Syllabus breakdowns and key information for leading entrance examinations.

PG Management (MBA)

CAT (Common Admission Test)

VARC, DILR, Quant. The prime pathway for admissions into 20+ Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs).

GMAT Focus Edition

Quantitative, Verbal, and Data Insights. Required for executive programs and global MBA pathways.

GRE General Test

Verbal Reasoning, Quant Reasoning, and Analytical Writing. Accepted for global MS/MBA programs.

NMAT by GMAC

Language, Quantitative, and Logical Skills. Custom entrance for NMIMS Mumbai and leading private business schools.

IIM Bangalore DBE

Specialized digital transformation, entrepreneurship, product management, and analytics entrance metrics.

UG Admissions (BBA/CUET)

IPMAT (IIM Indore & Rohtak)

Integrated Program in Management Aptitude Test. Direct pathway to the prestigious 5-year BBA+MBA integrated program.

JIPMAT (IIM Jammu & Bodh Gaya)

Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test. Joint evaluation on QA, DILR, and Verbal.

SET (Symbiosis Entrance Test)

Evaluates English, Quantitative, GK, and analytical reasoning for Symbiosis undergraduate BBA admissions.

Christ University ET

Entrance test testing English, GK, Math, Logic, and Data Analysis for Christ University BBA/B.Com.

CUET (Common UG Test)

National-level single window entrance for central university undergraduate admissions (DU, BHU, JNU, etc.).

Design & Law Specialties

UCEED (IIT Design Pathway)

Undergraduate Common Entrance Examination for Design. Pathway for B.Des at IIT Bombay, Guwahati, Hyderabad.

DAT (Design Aptitude Test)

National Institute of Design (NID) entrance. Evaluates color theory, sketching ability, and visual thinking.

CLAT (Common Law Test)

Pathway for India's National Law Universities (NLUs). Evaluates comprehension, legal logic, and current affairs.
