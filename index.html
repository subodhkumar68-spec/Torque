Business & Tech Management

Careers include Product Managers, Strategy Consultants, and HR Operations directors.

Entrance: CAT, GMAT, IPMAT | Avg. Placements: ₹12L - ₹28L per annum.

Best Resources & Readings to Go Through:

Recommended Reading Materials
  • "The McKinsey Way" by Ethan M. Rasiel - Master structured business problem-solving frameworks.
  • "Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love" by Marty Cagan - The holy grail guide for modern Tech Product Managers.
Interactive Learning Portals
  • Mindler Career Blog: Essential resource for stream selection and career roadmap reviews. Visit Blog
  • Harvard Business Review (HBR): Premium business case studies, case-cracking tutorials, and industry reports. Open HBR
  • Strategy+Business: Highly recommended for reading management trends and corporate strategy notes. Explore Portal
Banking, Finance & Accounts

Careers include Investment Bankers, Wealth Managers, Actuaries, and Treasury Officers.

Entrance: CAT, GMAT, IPMAT | Avg. Placements: ₹10L - ₹32L per annum.

Best Resources & Readings to Go Through:

Recommended Reading Materials
  • "Investment Banking: Valuation, LBOs, M&A" by Joshua Rosenbaum & Joshua Pearl - The foundational textbook for financial analysis & modeling.
  • "The Intelligent Investor" by Benjamin Graham - The definitive book on value investing and asset allocation.
Interactive Learning Portals
  • Corporate Finance Institute (CFI): Great resource for free templates, modeling sheets, and finance lessons. Visit CFI
  • Investopedia: The most comprehensive interactive dictionary for financial concepts, ratios, and tutorials. Open Investopedia
  • Bloomberg Markets: Real-time macro analysis, market briefings, and global financial reviews. Open Bloomberg
Computer Science & AI Analytics

Careers include Data Scientists, Software Architects, and Prompt Engineers.

Target Skills: Python, LLMs, SQL, Agile | Avg. Placements: ₹14L - ₹35L per annum.

Best Resources & Readings to Go Through:

Recommended Reading Materials
  • "Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship" by Robert C. Martin - Essential principles of writing clean, maintainable engineering code.
  • "Designing Data-Intensive Applications" by Martin Kleppmann - The industry benchmark guide for learning system design, databases, and scaling.
Interactive Learning Portals
  • Kaggle: Curated machine learning datasets, data science competitions, and interactive notebooks. Visit Kaggle
  • GitHub Explore: Great for discovering trending codebases, open-source libraries, and prompt repos. Open GitHub Explore
  • DeepLearning.AI: Leading courses in machine learning, LLM fine-tuning, and prompt engineering by Andrew Ng. Explore DeepLearning
