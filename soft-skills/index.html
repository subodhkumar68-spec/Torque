                                 Peer Group Work & Listening
                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    Effective collaboration thrives on active listening. Master the 3-Second Pause rule: before responding to a peer in group work, pause for 3 seconds to ensure they have finished explaining their point. It reduces interruptions by 40% and builds respect.
                                

                            

                        


                        

                            

                                 Executive Presentation Delivery
                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    Deliver high-impact presentations using the 10-20-30 Rule: 
                                    
• Maximum of 10 slides
                                    
• Delivery in under 20 minutes
                                    
• Font size no smaller than 30 points for high readability.
                                

                            

                        

                    

                


                
                

                    
 MBA Soft Skills

                    
Advanced corporate leadership and communication modules.

                    
                    

                        

                            

                                 Minto Pyramid Principle
                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    Structured executive communication: state your conclusion first, then group your supporting arguments logically, and finally present the underlying data.
                                

                                

  [Conclusion / Recommendation]
         ▲
         │ (Why? / How?)
  [Argument A] ── [Argument B] ── [Argument C]
         ▲
         │ (Supported by)
  [Data Pillar] ── [Data Pillar] ── [Data Pillar]
                                

                            

                        


                        

                            

                                 B-School GD Consensus Building
                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    Group Discussions (GD) are crucial for business school admissions. Stand out by playing the Synthesizer role: instead of repeating your arguments, summarize conflicting views and suggest a consensus framework (e.g., "Since we disagree on marketing channels, let's allocate 60% to digital and test offline in v2.0").
                                

                            

                        


                        

                            

                                 Advanced Business Negotiation
                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    Always establish your BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement) and Reservation Price before entering a meeting. Leverage collaborative variable packaging: trade terms of lower value to you but higher value to them (e.g. payment terms vs unit price) to achieve win-win deals.
